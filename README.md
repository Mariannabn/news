# 🎮 Tech & Gaming News

Hiii! 👋

I built a simple news scraper that collects the latest news from the worlds of **gaming and technology** in one place.

Instead of checking three different websites separately, you can browse the latest stories, find something that catches your interest, and jump straight to the original article with just one click.

The goal is simple: **less searching, more discovering!** 🚀
